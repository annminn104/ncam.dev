# CD Player: the art side

All four models, the suitcase, the Clear model (an upright, see-through player), the Deck (a DJ CD
deck) and Luxe (a turntable), are generated by Python running inside Blender, not modelled by
hand.

- `blender/scripts/cdkit.py`: geometry helpers (2D slabs with rounded edges, tubes, lathes, a
  partial lathe for the corner caps, stitches, text) and Principled materials, in millimetres;
  the Cycles-only quilt and weave bumps; Metal and a headless render.
- `blender/scripts/parts.py`: the CD, the crystal jewel case and the player: its hardware (corner
  caps, keyhole clasps, rivets, nameplate, grille frame), piping and stitching, the handle with
  its luggage tag, the deck's chrome ring, screws and VU strip, the tonearm, the quilted lid with
  its strap, the five-slot rack and the plate. `build_materials(palette)` makes every material;
  the seven the skins recolour take their colours from a palette of sRGB hex values (`BLUSH` by
  default).
- `blender/scripts/build_player.py`: the web scene, with the names the page drives
  (`src/scene/contract.ts`).
- `blender/scripts/clear.py`: the Clear model, on `parts.py`'s disc and case: a clear
  polycarbonate shell (front and back plates and one hollow band, never a filled block) on a
  plinth, the round glass door hinged on its left, the disc on its spindle ringed by the light
  ring, the swing arm with its voice coil, the spindle motor's copper coils, the speakers, four AA
  cells, the keys and their own board, the knob standing upright on the top beside them, the
  handle and its tag, the nameplate, the stand with the rack's five slots and the standing case,
  put together by `make_clear_player`, with the board and the decorations below. It holds the
  model's dimensions and helpers (the paths on a face that the board and the decorations share
  among them); `clear_look(spec)` reads a look from `src/clear-looks.json` (and stops the run,
  naming what is wrong, on a part or a field it lacks) and `build_clear_materials(look)` makes
  its materials.
- `blender/scripts/clear_board.py`: the Clear model's board (the SoC under its heat spreader, two
  LPDDR packages, the PMIC and its inductors, USB-C, the Wi-Fi module and its meander antenna, six
  LEDs, test pads, 45-degree copper traces with a serpentine and a hexagon pour, the LCD, the VU
  strip, the Đông Sơn sun beside the silkscreened logo), built by `clear_board` and laid out by
  its `Board`, which AGENTS.md describes.
- `blender/scripts/clear_decor.py`: the Clear model's decorations, each a `Relief` (AGENTS.md
  describes it): frosted circuit-maze etching on the shell's sides and top (`clear_etch`), a gold
  Đông Sơn ring round the door (`clear_dongson_ring`: tangent circles, a hatched ladder, eight Lạc
  birds) and hatched lozenge chains in relief on the plinth's front and the keys' deck
  (`clear_grooves`). It and `clear_board.py` read `clear.py`'s names, so `export.py` and `hero.py`
  exec both after it, into the same namespace.
- `blender/scripts/build_clear.py`: the Clear model's web scene, with the names the page drives
  (`src/scene/contract.ts`) and the door's `door_hinge`.
- `blender/scripts/deck.py`: the Deck, a top-loading DJ CD deck on `parts.py`'s disc and case: the
  body with the jog's well, the clear acrylic jog plate hinged over the disc, the LED ring round
  the jog, the arm and the keys; `make_deck_player` puts the set together, `deck_look(spec)` reads
  a look from `src/deck-looks.json` and `build_deck_materials(look)` makes its materials.
- `blender/scripts/deck_panel.py`: the Deck's screen module, decor controls (pads, FX knobs,
  encoder, tempo fader), printed labels and the body's screws, ports and vents.
- `blender/scripts/deck_case.py`: the Deck's flight case with its five foam slots, and its jewel
  case.
- `blender/scripts/deck_print.py`: the Deck's print layer (the zones the page paints its pattern
  and name on), and the atlas layout it writes to `src/scene/textures/deck-atlas.json`.
- `blender/scripts/build_deck.py`: the Deck's web scene, with the names the page drives
  (`src/scene/contract.ts`), its jog plate's `door_hinge` and its `jog_ring`.
- `blender/scripts/vinyl.py`: what every turntable shares: the 12-inch record (vinyl and its
  label), its sleeve and the LED ring round the platter.
- `blender/scripts/luxe.py`: Luxe, on `parts.py` and `vinyl.py`: a lacquered two-tier plinth, the
  platter and its mat, the keys, and the colours of `src/luxe-looks.json` (`luxe_look(spec)`,
  `build_luxe_materials(look)`); `make_luxe_player` puts the set together.
- `blender/scripts/luxe_arm.py`: Luxe's tonearm (base, bearing, rest post, cue lever, the J tube,
  head and needle) and its two angles, `LUXE_ARM_LEAD_IN` and `LUXE_ARM_RUN_OUT`.
- `blender/scripts/luxe_stand.py`: Luxe's record stand (five steps with the next five records in
  their sleeves) and the ledge behind the platter.
- `blender/scripts/build_luxe.py`: Luxe's web scene, with the names a turntable needs
  (`src/scene/contract.ts`) and the arm's angles printed for the page's profile.
- `blender/scripts/export.py`: the background entry. It builds the model `CDP_MODEL` names
  (`suitcase`, the default, `clear`, `deck` or `luxe`), meshes curves and text, adds UVs, and writes the raw GLB.
- `blender/scripts/hero.py`: the Cycles stills, headless: `CDP_MODEL=suitcase|clear|deck|luxe`,
  `CDP_HERO_VIEW=hero|lid` (the suitcase), `hero|inside` (the Clear model), `hero|top` (the Deck)
  or `hero|platter` (Luxe; `CDP_LUXE_LOOK=<body>,<metal>,<light>` picks its look),
  `CDP_HERO_QUALITY=preview|final`, into `.out/v2-<view>-<quality>.png`, or
  `.out/clear/<shell>-<frame>-<board>-<light>/clear-<view>-<quality>.png` for the Clear model
  (`CDP_CLEAR_LOOK`, below), whose studio also sees the backdrop through the shell
  (`see_through_backdrop`) and sets dark reflection flags round it (`clear_flags`). For the owner's
  gates and the docs only.
- `blender/tools/bl.py`: talks to a running Blender through the Blender Lab MCP add-on's socket
  (127.0.0.1:9876, `{"type": "execute", "code": …}` + NUL). Use it for interactive design work;
  the build never needs it.

## Build

Needs Blender 5.2.2 LTS (`/Applications/Blender.app`, or `BLENDER_BIN`).

    pnpm --filter @ncam/cd-player art:build         # the suitcase
    pnpm --filter @ncam/cd-player art:build:clear   # the Clear model
    pnpm --filter @ncam/cd-player art:build:deck    # the Deck
    pnpm --filter @ncam/cd-player art:build:luxe    # Luxe, the turntable

They write `public/assets/cd-player.glb`, `public/assets/cd-player-clear.glb`,
`public/assets/cd-player-deck.glb` and `public/assets/cd-player-luxe.glb` (all committed;
`pipeline/build.mjs` hands the model to `export.py` as `CDP_MODEL`).
`src/scene/model-contract.test.ts` checks each one's names, materials, where things are parented
and the budget (150k triangles, 4 MB). The committed suitcase is about 100,633 triangles and
776 KB, the Clear model about 132,715 and 965 KB, the Deck about 81,762 and 702 KB, the Luxe
turntable about 28,668 and 250 KB (KB of 1024 bytes). Rebuild a model after any change to its scripts
(`cdkit.py` and `parts.py` are every model's, `vinyl.py` every turntable's; the Deck's build also
rewrites `src/scene/textures/deck-atlas.json`, which is committed with the GLB).

## Renders

    CDP_HERO_VIEW=hero CDP_HERO_QUALITY=preview \
      /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup \
      --python-exit-code 1 --python blender/scripts/hero.py

The first Cycles run on Metal compiles its kernels for about two minutes. The renders are not in
the repository.

## Skins

`src/skins.json`, the file the page reads, holds every skin's palette; `parts.py` reads Blush
from it as `BLUSH`, the default of `build_materials()`. `CDP_SKIN=<id>` (an id of that file)
renders another skin with `hero.py`, into `.out/skins/<id>/v2-<view>-<quality>.png`:

    CDP_SKIN=sage CDP_HERO_VIEW=hero CDP_HERO_QUALITY=final \
      /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup \
      --python-exit-code 1 --python blender/scripts/hero.py

The skins change only the seven materials named after the skin roles; every other material keeps
the colour `build_materials()` gives it.

## The Clear model

`src/clear-looks.json`, the file the page reads, holds the Clear model's colours part by part:
the shell's tint, the frame (the plinth, the door's bezel, the keys, the handle, the speaker cones,
the stand's cheeks), the board and the light (the ring round the disc, the VU strip, the keys'
icons, the board's six LEDs). The play key carries two icons, ▶ and ‖ (`button_icon("play")` and `("pause")` in
`parts.py`), built as `icon_play` / `icon_pause` in both models' web scenes and shown one at a
time by the page; a render, which plays, prints ‖ alone. `CDP_MODEL=clear` renders it in its
default look, or in the one
`CDP_CLEAR_LOOK=<shell>,<frame>,<board>,<light>` names (ids of that file), into
`.out/clear/<shell>-<frame>-<board>-<light>/clear-<view>-<quality>.png` (`CDP_SKIN` is the
suitcase's alone: with the Clear model it stops the run, as `CDP_CLEAR_LOOK` does with the
suitcase); `hero` is the three-quarter still between the stand and the case, `inside` a close-up of
the board and the mechanism through the shell:

    CDP_MODEL=clear CDP_CLEAR_LOOK=smoke,black,black,amber CDP_HERO_VIEW=inside \
      CDP_HERO_QUALITY=final /Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background \
      --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python blender/scripts/hero.py

A render plays: the arm over the disc, discs in the rack, the LCD and seven VU segments lit, ‖
alone on the play key, the plate and the tag lettered. The web scene (`build_clear.py`) is at
rest: the door shut, the arm off the disc's rim, the rack's slots empty, the prints as plain planes
for the page to paint.
